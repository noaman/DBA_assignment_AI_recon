Business intent is not in the code

Why it was written — the regulatory rule, product variation, or workaround — is rarely documented and often known only to consultants who have moved on.

Release notes are unstructured

Flexcube 14.8 release notes run to several thousand pages of narrative text. No rule-based system can reliably cross-reference at that volume.

Institutional memory walks out

Multi-year programmes cycle through staff. By upgrade time, the people who built the customisations are gone. AI must reason from artefacts alone.

Act 2 · The Intellectual Pivot

Why AI — and Not Conventional Automation

This is not an automation problem. It is a reasoning problem — and reasoning at scale is what large language models now do.

What Conventional Automation Can Do

  • ✕ Match patterns in structured data
  • ✕ Apply predefined rules across known categories
  • ✕ Process high volumes within defined scope

What This Problem Requires

  • ✓ Understand business intent from unstructured code and partial documentation
  • ✓ Exercise judgement in ambiguous situations where no rule exists
  • ✓ Reason across 3,000+ pages of narrative release notes simultaneously

LLMs cross this gap — read and reason across code, documentation, and release notes; infer business intent from context; produce structured recommendations with rationale and source citations at volume no consultant team can match.
